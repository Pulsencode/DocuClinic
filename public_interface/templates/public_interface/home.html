Everything your clinic needs

From front-desk scheduling to physician prescriptions — manage daily operations in one place.

Patient Management

Store profiles, vitals, allergies, and emergency contacts. Auto-calculated BMI and age from date of birth.

Appointments

Schedule visits, track status, apply discounts, and align bookings with physician availability and clinic hours.

Medical Records

Create prescriptions with diagnosis, medicines, dosage, and follow-up dates — linked to your inventory.

Inventory

Track medicine stock, expiry dates, suppliers, and storage locations with min/max level alerts.

Role-Based Access

Separate workflows for administrators, physicians, nurses, receptionists, and patients — each with the right tools.

Clinic Settings

Configure your clinic profile, logo, license details, GST, and consultation duration from one central place.
